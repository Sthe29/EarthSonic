EarthSonic Architectural Principles

Meaningful Sonification

Auditory pitch and modulation directly map to physical units (such as degrees Celsius), converting scientific complexity into intuitive sound.

OpenCV Computer Vision

Server-side image analysis processes regions of interest in milliseconds using CIE L*a*b* nearest-gradient colormap matching.

Universal Accessibility

Built with ARIA live regions, keyboard navigation, and auditory icons to empower blind and low-vision researchers.
