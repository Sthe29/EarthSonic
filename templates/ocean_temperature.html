Scientific Data Provenance & Dataset Information

Visualization Source

NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) ID 5101: Sea Surface Temperature — Near Real Time. Global equirectangular projection (1024×512 resolution).

Visit SVS 5101 on NASA.gov ↗
Underlying Dataset

JPL MUR Sea Surface Temperature Analysis (GHRSST Level 4 MUR Global Foundation SST v4.1). Multi-sensor blended analysis at 0.01° (~1 km) resolution.

Visit PO.DAAC MUR Dataset ↗
Measurement Discipline

Measures daily Foundation Sea Surface Temperature only. Does not depict ocean currents, surface winds, atmospheric precipitation, or forecasts.

Calibration Method

Calibrated using OpenCV CIE L*a*b* Euclidean distance matching against the NASA legend (-5°C to 35°C). Experimental visualization estimate; not raw sensor arrays.
